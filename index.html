How the plan works

We aim for up to three sightings of each racer with at most four stops. The suggestion prioritizes seeing everyone once, then everyone twice, then a third time. Travel time breaks ties. It is a balanced suggestion, not a guaranteed optimum.

With no pins we choose viewing spots near Indego stations. Pinning stations limits viewing spots to those stations. Start-to-finish repeat passes are considered automatically.

Racer dots and arrival times are pace estimates, refined by any split you enter. The course trace and mile locations are approximate. We allow 3 minutes before and after a sighting. Transfers assume 10 mph cycling or 3 mph walking, straight-line distance × 1.5, plus 5 minutes for bike pickup/docking. Verify crossings and directions on race day. The first trip is not included.

The official live view runs in RTRT’s own interface; its favorites and live data do not sync to this planner. Their API requires approved application access. This app does not reuse credentials or claim that estimates are GPS.

Plans stay on this device. A shared link includes a snapshot of racer names, bibs, paces, splits and pins; later edits need a new link.
