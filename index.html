About this map & sources

The green line is an approximate trace of the official 2026 half marathon map, not a turn-by-turn navigation route. Distances are straight-line estimates from that trace. Bridges, park slopes, barriers and race closures can change access.

Bike counts include e-bikes. Refreshes every 60 seconds while this page is open. Counts older than five minutes are marked stale. Availability today does not confirm race-day operation.
